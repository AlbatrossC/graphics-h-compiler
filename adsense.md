# AdSense "Low Value Content" Rejection — Full Analysis & Fix Guide

> **Context:** Comprehensive analysis of why Google AdSense keeps rejecting sites with "Low Value Content" and a step-by-step fix roadmap. Sources: Official Google AdSense documentation, Google AdSense Community forums (16K+ view pinned thread), Reddit (r/Blogging, r/blogs, r/Bloggers), Digital Point Forums, and dev.to publisher experiences (2024–2026).

---

## What "Low Value Content" Actually Means

"Low Value Content" is Google's **catch-all rejection code**. It rarely means just bad writing. It means Google's automated system couldn't find enough trust signals across your entire site. One weak area can fail the whole review.

The reported AdSense application rejection rate is approximately **95–96%**. This is not a personalised critique — it is a system-level gate.

Triggers include:
- Weak or missing E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) signals
- Site too new (informal 3–6 month age threshold)
- Zero or very low organic traffic
- AI-generated content with no human value-add
- Thin pages dragging down an otherwise decent site
- Affiliate links present during review
- Missing or shallow required pages (About, Privacy, Contact)
- Scraped or rewritten content (even if it passes plagiarism checkers)

## Hidden Causes Most People Miss

### 1. Site Age

Google informally expects 3–6 months of domain history before trusting a new site. Developers have reported being rejected with solid content, proper required pages, and original writing — simply because the domain was too new.

> *"Low value content is basically Google's catch-all rejection for new sites. Site age — Google wants 3-6 months of history before they trust you."*
> — dev.to publisher (Dec 2025)

### 2. AI Content Without Human Value-Add

With billions of AI-generated pages flooding the web, Google's crawlers now actively look for **information gain** — does your content add something no other page has?

- If content is ~95% AI with no personal perspective, original data, or genuine experience: **expect rejection**
- The 50/50 rule: at least 50% of the value should come from your own unique contribution
- AI content is detected not by AI detectors, but by the *absence* of originality — no opinions, no personal anecdotes, no information that couldn't come from any other source

### 3. Information Redundancy

> *"If 100,000 other sites have the exact same steps, your post is low-value by default — even if it's well written. It's not that your post is bad; it's just that the internet doesn't need another copy of it."*
> — Adstimate analysis (March 2026)

### 4. Weak About Page / No Author Expertise

A generic "We're passionate bloggers" about page is a rejection signal. This is one of the most commonly cited issues in community reviews of rejected sites.

### 5. Traffic Threshold (Unofficial)

No official minimum exists, but community experience consistently points to **30–100 organic visitors/day** as a soft threshold that significantly improves approval odds. Zero traffic = high rejection risk.

## Step-by-Step Fix Checklist

### Step 1 — Content Audit (Delete or Upgrade Thin Pages)

- [ ] Identify all pages under 500 words
- [ ] Delete pages with no real insight, or expand them significantly
- [ ] Remove auto-generated pages, tag pages, empty category pages
- [ ] Fix or remove any pages with domain redirect issues (check Search Console)
- [ ] Target: 15–25 strong articles beats 50 mediocre ones

### Step 2 — Add Information Gain to Every Article

- [ ] Add personal experience, real numbers, or case studies
- [ ] Replace generic tips ("5 ways to save money") with specific real examples ("How I saved ₹8,000 in February by switching these 3 apps")
- [ ] Add a "Last Updated" timestamp and actually update posts with fresh data
- [ ] Aim for 1,500–2,500+ words for cornerstone articles
- [ ] Make each article the best resource on the internet for that specific sub-topic

### Step 3 — Fix About Page & Author Bios (E-E-A-T)

- [ ] Rewrite About page with real credentials, specific experience, and personal story
- [ ] Add author boxes to every post with qualifications relevant to the niche
- [ ] For YMYL niches (health, finance, legal): demonstrate professional credentials explicitly
- [ ] Add real photos of yourself or your team — not stock avatars

### Step 4 — Ensure All Required Pages Exist and Are Indexed

- [ ] About Us — substantive, not a placeholder
- [ ] Privacy Policy — a real privacy policy, not 3 lines
- [ ] Contact Us — working contact form or email
- [ ] Terms of Service (recommended)
- [ ] Verify all are indexed in Google Search Console
- [ ] Ensure all are linked from footer/main navigation

### Step 5 — Fix or Heavily Edit AI Content

- [ ] Go through every AI-assisted post and add your own experience
- [ ] Add specific examples, original screenshots, or real-world data
- [ ] Rewrite introductions and conclusions in your own voice
- [ ] Remove any content that reads as generic or could have been written by anyone
- [ ] If unsure, read each post and ask: "Does this have information only I could provide?"

### Step 6 — Use Original Images

- [ ] Replace stock photos with your own photos, screenshots, or diagrams
- [ ] A personal photo (even low quality) is more valuable to Google than a polished stock image
- [ ] Ensure all images are high-resolution and load correctly
- [ ] Add descriptive alt text to all images

### Step 7 — Remove Affiliate Links & Other Ad Networks Before Applying

- [ ] Remove all affiliate links (Amazon, ShareASale, etc.) before submitting
- [ ] Remove any other ad network code (Monetag, Adsterra, etc.)
- [ ] Restore after AdSense approval

### Step 8 — Technical & UX Checks

- [ ] Site is mobile-friendly (test at search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly)
- [ ] Page speed is acceptable (test at pagespeed.web.dev)
- [ ] No broken links or 404 errors
- [ ] Clean navigation — clear menu, categories, internal links
- [ ] No aggressive pop-ups blocking content
- [ ] ads.txt file is in place (if you have other ad networks — remove those first)
- [ ] URL structure is clean and logical

### Step 9 — Wait, Then Reapply

- [ ] Wait **minimum 2–4 weeks** after making significant changes
- [ ] Verify changes are re-crawled and re-indexed in Search Console
- [ ] Keep publishing 1–2 new quality posts per week during the wait
- [ ] Total recommended timeline: **6–10 weeks** from rejection to reapplication

## Summary

The "Low Value Content" rejection is not always about content quality in isolation. It is about the **total trust profile** of your site. Fix E-E-A-T signals, eliminate thin and AI-only content, prove your expertise on your About page, remove monetisation signals before applying, and wait long enough for Google to see your improvements. Most people who finally get approved say the same thing: they had to fix more than they thought, and they had to wait longer than they wanted.
